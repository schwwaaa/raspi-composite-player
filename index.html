The whole procedure

Five steps. No desktop. No daily operator.

The image handles the awkward legacy setup so the finished object behaves more like a DVD player than a Linux computer.

  1. 01

    Download

    Grab and uncompress the prepared disk image.

  2. 02

    Flash

    Write it to an SD card with your preferred imaging tool.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Use a TRRS-to-RCA cable from the Pi 3B+ to your CRT.

  4. 04

    Insert

    Put the SD card in the Pi and connect power.

  5. 05

    Watch

    The demo starts automatically and loops continuously.

Autoplay by default

Playback starts after boot with no login, mouse, window manager, or media-player interface.

Simple playlists

Drop MP4s into /home/mediaplayer/media. Files loop in ascending alphanumeric order.

⌨

Physical controls

Connect any USB keyboard or numpad for pause, seek, next, previous, and quit controls.

Feed the CRT properly

Pre-encode once. Play smoothly forever.

Composite playback is happiest when the Pi does not have to resize or reinterpret modern high-resolution files in real time.
